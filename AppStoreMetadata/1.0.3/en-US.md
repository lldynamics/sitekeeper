# Sitekeeper 1.0.3

## Promotional Text

Organize projects, site logs, to-dos, materials, quotes, receipts, and drawing files. Find records with global search and protect them with local ZIP or iCloud backup.

## What's New

- Added global search across projects, daily logs, to-dos, and quick notes.
- Added optional due reminders for to-dos and project financial records.
- Added quick entry on the dashboard for to-dos, quick notes, and bookkeeping records.
- Improved file search, list loading, and data persistence for larger projects.
- Strengthened local ZIP and iCloud backups with integrity checks, pre-restore validation, and corruption warnings.
- Improved iPhone and iPad layouts and navigation stability.
- Fixed known issues and improved overall reliability.

Note: Electrical, measurement, and quantity calculation results are estimates only. On-site work should follow applicable standards, design documents, and professional judgment.

## App Review Notes

Sitekeeper is an on-site construction project management utility that does not require registration or login.

Suggested review path:
1. Open Projects, create a project, and review its dashboard, daily logs, to-dos, and files.
2. Use dashboard quick entry to create a to-do, quick note, or bookkeeping record.
3. Use global search to find projects, logs, to-dos, and quick notes.
4. Open Profile > Data Security Center to review local ZIP export/import and the optional iCloud backup flow.
5. Open Profile > Settings to opt in to due reminders. Denying notification permission does not affect other features.

Data is stored locally by default. iCloud backup writes only to the user's private iCloud Documents container after an explicit user action or opt-in to automatic backup. The app contains no advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking.

DWG and DXF files are managed as original drawing records with version and backup support. The app hands these originals to an external professional viewer selected by the user and does not claim full in-app CAD rendering or editing.

Feedback is sent through the system mail composer. The app has no account system and no review account is required.

Electrical, measurement, and quantity calculation results are estimates only. On-site work should follow applicable standards, design documents, and professional judgment.
